# About DataTrace

**DataTrace** helps people organize privacy-related evidence—such as emails and documents—into a structured case record. Its goal is to connect requests, responses, and supporting evidence, then prepare clear, evidence-grounded reports for the user to review.

## Website

[Visit DataTrace](https://data-trace-kappa.vercel.app/)

## Project status

DataTrace is a work in progress. The repository includes backend, ingestion, AI gateway, and verification components, but the complete user-facing workflow is not yet implemented. See the [README](README.md) for current implementation details.

DataTrace is intended to support evidence review and report preparation; it does not determine legal compliance or provide legal advice.
